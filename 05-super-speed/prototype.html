What this prototype assumes

This scenario assumes verified ranking traces show low acceptance history contributing to Mite’s low offer count. It is not a diagnosis of the historical case. Production must distinguish low demand, capability mismatch, availability and delivery issues.

Proposed flow: Kip requests a readiness check; Mite confirms; routing temporarily uses a neutral history contribution for suitable incidents. Availability and capability requirements still apply. The demonstration ends that treatment after one offer outcome. Eligibility thresholds, duration, repeat use, precise ranking rules and treatment of declines versus misses require Product and Engineering agreement.

The displayed counts are invented examples. No responder-record schema changes are specified.
