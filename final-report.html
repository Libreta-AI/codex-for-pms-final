The prevention habit / Mondays at 9:00 a.m. Chicago

Make missing decisions
harder to overlook.

review-checklist turns a one-off review into a repeatable practice. It flags weak briefs with evidence and small, specific fixes; a human owns the decision.

01 / Name the ownerWho is accountable for resolving the decision?
02 / Define successWhat outcome, target and observation window show it worked?
03 / Keep scope consistentDo the opening promise and final deliverables agree?
04 / Problem before fixWhose problem are we solving, and why does it matter?

This reduces the risk of shipping an under-specified response. It cannot prevent a routing regression by itself: production instrumentation, technical review and outcome monitoring are still required. No scheduled review result has been verified in this session; the course's four-flags output is an illustration.
